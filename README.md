# 🦖 Dino Discovery Quiz Game

An **interactive educational quiz game** featuring a cute dinosaur mascot, multiple difficulty levels, background music, and Discord Rich Presence integration.

---

## ✨ Features

```markdown
- Interactive Quiz Game – Engaging educational quiz with three difficulty levels: Easy, Normal, Hard
- Cute Dinosaur Mascot – Adorable dinosaur companion guides users through the experience
- Multi-Level Gameplay – Progressive difficulty: 5 Easy, 5 Normal, 10 Hard questions
- Background Music – Immersive audio experience with adjustable volume controls
- Settings Menu – Customizable volume slider for personalized audio settings
- Credits Screen – Recognition of contributors
- Discord Rich Presence – Shows game status on Discord
```

---

## 🎮 Quiz Categories

```markdown
- Science – Physics, Chemistry, Biology
- Geography
- History & Literature
- General Knowledge
```
